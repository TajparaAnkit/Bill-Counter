# Bill Counter — User Guide

Welcome to **Bill Counter**, a simple billing and invoicing app for small businesses. This guide explains — in plain language — what you can do with it and how, so you can start billing customers in minutes.

---

## What is Bill Counter?

Bill Counter is an online tool that helps you:

- Keep a list of your **products** and their prices
- Save your **customers** and their contact details
- Create clean, professional **invoices** with a few taps
- Let customers **pay by scanning a UPI QR code**
- **Share invoices on WhatsApp** instantly
- Track who has **paid** and who still **owes** you money
- See your **sales at a glance** on a dashboard

Everything is saved securely to the cloud, so your data is safe and available on any device.

---

## Who is it for?

Shops, home businesses, freelancers, resellers, service providers — anyone who needs to hand a bill to a customer and keep track of payments, without the complexity of heavy accounting software.

---

## Getting Started

1. **Create your account** — open the app, click **Create an account**, and sign up with your email (or use **Continue with Google**). Enter your business name.
2. **Set up your business** — go to **Settings** (top-right menu) and add:
   - Your business name, address, and phone → these appear on every invoice
   - Your **UPI ID** (e.g. `yourname@okhdfcbank`) → so customers can scan and pay
   - An **invoice prefix** (e.g. `INV`) if you want your bills numbered your way
   - **GST details**, only if your business is GST-registered (optional)
3. You're ready to bill!

> **Tip:** Forgot your password? On the login screen, type your email and click **Forgot password?** — you'll get a reset link.

## Everyday Use Cases

### 🧾 Create and give a customer their bill
1. Open **Bills & Invoices** → **New Bill**.
2. Type the customer's name (saved customers auto-suggest as you type).
3. Add your items — start typing a product name and the price fills in automatically, or type any item manually. Set the quantity.
4. Add a **discount** if you like (a flat ₹ amount or a %), and **tax** if GST is enabled.
5. Click **Save Bill**. The invoice opens instantly.
6. Hand it over: **Download PDF**, or tap **Share** to send it on **WhatsApp**.

### 💳 Let a customer pay by scanning
- If you've set your **UPI ID** in Settings, every invoice shows a **Scan to pay** QR code with the exact amount.
- The customer opens any UPI app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm…), scans it, and pays — no typing amounts.

### 📌 Track who still owes you
- Every invoice has a status: **Paid**, **Partial**, or **Unpaid**.
- Open an invoice and use **Mark as → Paid / Partial / Unpaid**. For a partial payment, enter how much you received.
- The **Dashboard** shows your total **outstanding dues** and how many invoices are paid vs. unpaid.

### 📦 Manage your product list
- Go to **Product Manager** to add products with a name, price, and photo.
- Have a big list? Use **Import** to add many products at once from a spreadsheet (CSV) or JSON.
- Products you save show up as suggestions when creating invoices.

### 👥 Keep a customer directory
- Go to **Customers** to save names, phone numbers, and emails.
- Saved customers auto-fill on new invoices (including their phone, used for WhatsApp sharing).

### 📊 Check how your business is doing
- The **Dashboard** shows today's sales, total invoices, average order value, your **last 7 days** sales trend, recent transactions, top-selling products, and outstanding payments.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need to install anything?**
No — it runs in your web browser. On a phone you can "Add to Home Screen" for an app-like shortcut.

**Is my data safe?**
Yes. Your data is stored in your own secure cloud account and is only visible to you when logged in.

**Can I use it without GST?**
Absolutely. GST/tax is **off by default** — turn it on in Settings only if you need it. Discounts are also optional.

**The QR code isn't a real payment code — why?**
The scan-to-pay QR only appears once you add your **UPI ID** in Settings. Until then, a placeholder image is shown.

**Can the customer receive the actual PDF on WhatsApp?**
On a phone, **Share** sends the PDF file directly. On a computer, the PDF downloads and WhatsApp opens so you can attach it (a limitation of WhatsApp on desktop).

**How do I change the business name on invoices?**
Update it in **Settings → Business / Brand Name**. It appears on all future invoices.
